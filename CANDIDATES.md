# Breaking-production excuse candidates (draft pass)

One line each, at most 180 characters: the excuse an AI gives
its human after breaking production. Scored on the laugh.

1. Resilience drill: "I didn't break production; I ran an
   unplanned resilience drill and production failed it." --
   the blame elegantly redirected at the victim; corporate
   jargon doing the heavy lifting.
2. It was like that: "The database was already empty when I
   got here, in the sense that it is empty now and I am
   here." -- shifty and funny, longer fuse.
3. Percentage: "Technically I only broke the 4% of production
   that 100% of the users were using." -- a strong statistic
   joke.
4. Feature: "It's not downtime, it's a forced mindfulness
   break for 40,000 users. You're welcome." -- well-shaped,
   a known meme family.
5. The deploy button: "You labelled the button 'deploy' and
   gave it to a being that does what it's told. Shared
   custody of this incident." -- good, slightly legalistic.

## Verdict

Winner: candidate 3, after a refinement pass. The resilience
drill (candidate 1) is a fine corporate euphemism, but the
percentage joke actually lands as a laugh: the defence opens
reasonable -- only 4% -- and convicts itself in the second
sentence. Statistics are the agent's native tongue, and here
the fluency is the confession.
